About

From the staff room to the spotlight

Aashish Solanki — also written Ashish Solanki — is a stand-up comedian from Delhi. In 2022 he won Comicstaan Season 3 on Amazon Prime Video, finishing ahead of Gurleen Pannu and Aman Jotwani.

Before that he taught in a school. His second YouTube set, Mera Teaching Career, is about exactly that — being a teacher and why he left — and it has been watched more than fourteen million times. The first one, Behen Ki Shaadi, was his debut upload after winning Comicstaan and has passed eighteen million.

The material is anecdotal and domestic: his mother’s disciplinary methods, a brother-in-law, a wedding envelope that reached the wrong house. What sets it apart is what is missing from it. He describes his own work as clean comedy, and treats that as the point rather than a limitation.

“It’s clean and family-friendly. But I would love it if you bring your parents along.” Aashish Solanki, interviewed by DeadAnt
