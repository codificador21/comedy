## Could NOT verify — deliberately left off the site

- **The exact name of the solo show.** Ticketing pages bill it as **"Good Boy, Better Show"**, but the
  DeadAnt interview — talking to him directly — calls it **"Good Boy, Better Jokes"**. The site uses
  the ticketed spelling because that is what an audience would search for. Worth confirming with him.
- **Instagram follower count.** Instagram serves a login wall. The 752K figure is from a search
  snippet; a third-party tracker (InstMe) reported 596.7K as of April 2026. The site says on the page
  that the number is approximate. There is also a second handle, `@aashishsolanki_1`, but his own
  channel and District both point to `@ashishsolanki_1`, which is the one used.
- **Dated upcoming shows.** His District artist page carried no live events on 27 Sep 2026, and the
  tour dates I could find (Kanpur 20 Sep, Lucknow 21 Sep, Pune 4–5 Apr) had passed. The Tour section
  links to his Linktree and District instead of printing expired dates.
- **"Five years in comedy"** appears in the DeadAnt piece, but the article is undated, so no start
  year is claimed on the site.
- **Age, date of birth, school or subject taught, real teaching employer.** Only aggregator biography
  sites carry these. Not used.
- **Total channel views.** Not exposed by YouTube without an API key; the fourth counter shows the
  views on his biggest single set instead.
- **International tour dates.** Australia / New Zealand / UAE come from a booking-agency bio, so the
  site mentions them only as past touring, with no dates or venues.
