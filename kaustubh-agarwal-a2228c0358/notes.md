## Could NOT verify — deliberately left off the site

- **Instagram follower count.** Instagram serves a login wall to non-logged-in requests. The 221K figure used in the counter comes from a Google search snippet for `@kaustubh_agarwal3`, not from Instagram itself. Treat it as approximate.
- **Total channel views.** YouTube no longer exposes this without an API key; the fourth counter shows the views on his biggest single upload instead, which is directly verifiable.
- **"Engineers Don't Cry" and "Desi Childhood Trauma"** are named as his sets in ticket-reseller copy (StubHub) and echoed in the District bio. No video, listing or recording of either exists that I could find, so they are not on the site.
- **Dated upcoming shows.** His District artist page existed but listed no live events on 20 Sep 2026. "Fully Trial Solo" appears as a past Delhi show title on reseller pages. The Shows section therefore links to live listing pages instead of printing dates that would go stale.
- **"Top 10 Comedian at IIT Delhi Rendezvous 2016", "co-founded Wannabe Idiot Techies", "Sales Manager at Trackier", "appeared on Pretty Good Roast Show"** — all from BrownPant only. The Pretty Good Roast appearance is in fact verifiable (video `e6ljc_caj8A` on Appurv Gupta's channel names "Kaustubh Aggarwal") and is used; the other three are not.
- **Age / date of birth.** Only aggregator sites give 1998; not used as a hard fact.
- **Spelling.** He is credited as both *Agarwal* and *Aggarwal*; the site uses Agarwal (his own channel's spelling) with *Aggarwal* in the schema `alternateName`.
