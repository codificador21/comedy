About

Kaustubh Agarwal is a Delhi-based stand-up comedian who performs primarily in Hindi. His sets are broadly dark, blue and observational — middle-class family life, relationships and the specific humiliations of growing up in a Delhi engineering pipeline, told with a flat, deadpan candour.

He has been on the circuit since 2016 and holds a B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Delhi Technological University. His solo show tours under the name Banger Issues.

Beyond his own sets he is a producer of formats. He created and hosts Akal Ke Ghode, a comedy quiz game show in which three comics guess awkward desi survey answers while a live audience backs a player and splits the winnings. He also co-created Loose Emotions with Vidit Sharma — an emotions-based live show where audience members bring real stories and comedians help normalise them.
