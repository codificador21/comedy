About

Bio

Vidit Sharma — on air and online as RJ Vidit — is a radio jockey turned stand-up comedian. His weekly Instagram routine takes the funniest news of the week apart; on stage he works in Hindi and English, in the observational storytelling lane, with a persona built around being permanently, articulately annoyed.

His solo show Namak Kam Hai has been touring India through 2026, playing rooms in Bengaluru, Chandigarh, Nagpur, Hyderabad, Bhopal, Kanpur, Lucknow and Indore, with tickets on BookMyShow.

He also co-created Loose Emotions with Kaustubh Agarwal — an emotions-based live format where audience members bring real stories and three comedians help them normalise it, with prizes for the funniest. The series has run past seventeen episodes on his channel.
