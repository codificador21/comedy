## Could NOT verify — deliberately left off the site

- **Which radio station.** A LinkedIn result title pairs his name with Radio Mirchi, but LinkedIn blocks fetching and no second source confirms it. The site says "radio jockey" and never names a station.
- **Booking email or phone.** He publishes neither. The booking form therefore copies a formatted enquiry to the clipboard for a DM to `@rjvidit` instead of pretending to email him.
- **Instagram follower count (295K).** From a search snippet, not Instagram — Instagram serves a login wall. One third-party tracker (Socialveins) shows a much lower 147.3K, so treat the figure as approximate and stale-prone.
- **Real name spelling / full name / age / hometown.** No reliable source found. Nothing claimed.
- **Dated future shows beyond Indore.** The September 2026 tour leg is documented city-by-city in press, but most of those dates had already passed by 20 Sep 2026. Only the Indore date (26 Sep) is printed; everything else links out.
- **Total channel views.** Not exposed by YouTube without an API key; the counter shows views on his biggest single set instead.
