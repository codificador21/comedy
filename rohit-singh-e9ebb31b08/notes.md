# Rohit Singh — research notes & sources

Research date: **20 September 2026**.

> **Disambiguation.** "Rohit Singh" is a common name and several comedians share it (Rohit Swain, Rohit Shah, Rohit Khurana, an actor Rohit Singh Matru). This site is about the Delhi stand-up whose channel is **youtube.com/@Rawhitsingh** (channel title "Rohit Singh", channel ID `UCWKctLwg6MkJ6BdEZn-yIig`) and whose Instagram, per his own video descriptions, is **@rohit.singh28**.

## Could NOT verify — deliberately left off the site

- **Instagram follower count.** No public figure found for `@rohit.singh28` or `@rawhitsingh`; Instagram blocks unauthenticated requests. The counters therefore show four YouTube-derived numbers and the site says explicitly why Instagram is absent.
- **Years active, age, hometown, education, real career history.** Nothing reliable exists. A Nerds of Comedy booking-agency page (https://nerdsofcomedy.com/team/rohit-singh/) describes a New Delhi "Rohit Singh" with "over a decade of performance experience" — that conflicts with a channel whose first standup upload is about two years old, so **it may be a different person and none of it is used**.
- **Which Instagram handle is primary.** Both `@rohit.singh28` (named in his own video descriptions and channel links) and `@rawhitsingh` exist. The site uses `@rohit.singh28` because he names it himself.
- **Dated upcoming shows.** He has no District or BookMyShow artist page. His Linktree is the only listing he maintains and it blocks automated fetching, so the Live section links to it rather than printing dates.
- **Brand sponsorships.** None found. The site has a "Selected credits" section (guest appearances, which are verifiable) instead of a brand-work section, rather than inventing partners.
- **"Rom rom" as a signature.** It opens the College Placement description in his own words; it is presented as his greeting, not as a branded catchphrase.
