I

About

Rohit Singh is a stand-up comedian working out of Delhi. His material sits squarely in the observational lane and rarely strays far from things that actually happened to him — a college placement and the life that follows it, riding a bike for a delivery app, the difference between a good day and a bad day at this job, and the newspaper.

He posts his sets to YouTube as @Rawhitsingh, shot in real Delhi rooms rather than studios: The Laugh Store and the Kedarnath Sahni Auditorium, with the Delhi circuit thanked by name in the credits of nearly every upload. One of those uploads is a crowd-work video built entirely around a heckler takedown.

Off his own channel he turns up across the Delhi NCR comedy ecosystem — Appurv Gupta’s Pretty Good Roast, Vivek Samtani’s Andha Pyaar, Kaustubh Agarwal’s Akal Ke Ghode and the panel show Judge Me If You Can. Live dates are collected on a single links page rather than a ticketing artist profile.
