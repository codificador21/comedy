- **The four TV show titles.** His own channel says "4 TV Shows" but does not name them. *The Kapil Sharma Show*, *Good Night India*, *Superstar Singers 2* and *Sau Karod Ka Kavi* come only from booking-agency and profile sites. Only *India's Laughter Champion* is independently confirmed (Wikipedia). IMDb (`nm14128264`) returned HTTP 403 and could not be checked.
- **"Began comedy in 2019", "2 lakh live audience", "50+ cities", "40 million viewers online".** All agency marketing copy. Not on the site.
- **Subscriber precision.** The channel page and the About panel returned 775K and 773K in the same session; the site uses 775K.
- **Total channel views.** Not exposed without an API key; the third counter uses the views on his biggest TV clip instead.
- **Booking phone number.** Publicly listed on his channel but deliberately not reproduced. Email only.
