The story

About

Rajat Sood is an Indian stand-up comedian, poetry artist, host, writer, songwriter, creative director and vlogger. In August 2022 he won India’s Laughter Champion on Sony Entertainment Television — a Banijay Asia production judged by Shekhar Suman and Archana Puran Singh and hosted by Rochelle Rao.

What he does on stage has its own name. Pomedy — poetry plus comedy — is his blend of sharp one-liners and romantic shayari, funny for most of a bit and then quietly devastating at the turn. Rough Copy, billed as India’s first Pomedy special, is the long-form version of it: an hour about a relationship that taught him how to flirt, how to speak better English, and how to teach a lesson.

“The most failed lover of this generation” — a stage persona built out of heartbreak, and the only one that could carry both the joke and the couplet.

Off stage he writes and releases music videos (Modern Majnu, King Life), runs the interview series Rajat Sood Unscripted, plays characters like Dilip Negi, and hosts. His channel lists him simply as Comedian | Poet | Entertainer | Host | Songwriter, with four television shows to his name.

He is currently touring I Am Worth It.
