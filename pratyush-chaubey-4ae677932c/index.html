About

Engineer se comedian tak

Pratyush Chaubey was born and brought up in a small town in Uttar Pradesh and spent his first career as a software engineer at TCS, a job that took him as far as Canada before it took his patience. He had written funny scripts and performed in plays through college; the switch finally landed when he watched comedian Appurv Gupta perform inside his own TCS office.

He left, and has been doing Hindi stand-up on the Indian circuit ever since. The comedy is anecdotal and lived-in — four brothers, inter-caste marriage, visa rejections, parenting, payment apps and the small indignities of being the responsible-looking one in every room. On the circuit he is known simply as Chaubey ji.

Most of his best-known specials were shot live at The Habitat, Mumbai, and his sets land with audiences on both sides of the generational line — college crowds and their parents.
