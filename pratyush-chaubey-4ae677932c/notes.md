## Could NOT verify — deliberately left off the site

- **Year he started comedy.** BrownPant says 2014; the Curly Tales interview gives no date. Not stated on the site.
- **Age (BrownPant says 38).** Aggregator-only. Not used.
- **Instagram follower count (205K).** From a search snippet, not Instagram itself (login wall). Approximate.
- **Dated upcoming shows.** The District listings I could find (9 May 2026 Delhi, 6 Jun 2026, 27 Jun 2026 Dehradun) had all passed by 20 Sep 2026, and he has no District *artist* page aggregating current dates. The Shows section links to live listing pages rather than printing expired dates.
- **Brand endorsements.** None found. The "Beyond the mic" section therefore covers venue, media, ticketing and promoter relationships, which are documented, and does not invent sponsorships.
- **Phone number.** His channel lists one publicly; it is deliberately not reproduced on the site. Email only.
- **Total channel views.** Not exposed without an API key; counter shows his biggest single upload instead.
